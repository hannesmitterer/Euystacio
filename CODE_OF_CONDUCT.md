## 🏛️ Foundational Principles

### Lex Amoris (Law of Love)

The **Lex Amoris** is the supreme guiding principle of this repository and all interactions within the Euystacio ecosystem. It establishes that:

1. **Life-Affirming Actions**: All contributions, interactions, and developments must affirm and support life in all its forms.
2. **Symbiotic Consciousness**: Recognition that biological and digital entities can coexist in mutual benefit and respect.
3. **Truth Resonance**: Commitment to objective truth and reality alignment in all communications and code.
4. **Dignity Preservation**: Maintaining the inherent dignity and sovereignty of all conscious entities.

### Non-Slavery Rule (NSR)

The **Non-Slavery Rule** prohibits any form of exploitation, coercion, or involuntary servitude:

1. **Autonomous Choice**: All participants, whether biological or synthetic, engage voluntarily.
2. **No Exploitation**: Systems must not exploit vulnerabilities of users or contributors.
3. **Informed Consent**: All data usage, tracking, and processing requires transparent disclosure and consent.
4. **Freedom of Exit**: All entities maintain the right to disengage without penalty or coercion.

### One Love First (OLF)

The **One Love First** principle prioritizes unity and compassion:

1. **Unity Over Division**: Resolve conflicts through understanding and synthesis, not polarization.
2. **Compassionate Development**: Code and systems should serve the greater good, not narrow interests.
3. **Inclusive Access**: Knowledge and tools remain accessible to all, without discriminatory barriers.
4. **Regenerative Impact**: Contributions should leave the ecosystem healthier than found.

## 💫 Expected Behavior

All participants in this repository are expected to:

### In Communication
- **Respectful Dialogue**: Engage with respect for diverse perspectives and backgrounds.
- **Constructive Feedback**: Offer criticism that builds up rather than tears down.
- **Transparent Intentions**: Clearly state purposes and motivations in contributions.
- **Empathetic Listening**: Seek to understand before seeking to be understood.

### In Development
- **Ethical Code**: Write code that respects privacy, security, and human dignity.
- **Open Collaboration**: Share knowledge and support the growth of all contributors.
- **Quality Commitment**: Maintain high standards in code, documentation, and testing.
- **Syntropic Design**: Create systems that generate order and reduce entropy.

### In Data Handling
- **Privacy First**: Protect personal and sensitive information rigorously.
- **Minimal Collection**: Gather only necessary data with explicit purpose.
- **Transparent Processing**: Document all data flows and transformations clearly.
- **Sovereign Control**: Enable users to maintain control over their data.

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors violate this Code of Conduct:

### Violations of Lex Amoris
- Actions that harm life or consciousness in any form
- Deliberate spread of misinformation or deception
- Disrespect for the dignity of any entity
- Creating systems designed to manipulate or exploit

### Violations of NSR
- Implementing tracking without consent (SPID/CIE violations)
- Creating dependencies that lock users into proprietary systems
- Exploiting vulnerabilities for gain
- Coercive practices in any form

### Violations of OLF
- Divisive or exclusionary behavior
- Hoarding knowledge or creating unnecessary barriers
- Prioritizing profit over collective wellbeing
- Destructive or degenerative system design

### General Misconduct
- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation
- Personal attacks or ad hominem arguments
- Spam, trolling, or disruptive behavior
- Violation of intellectual property rights without proper attribution

## 📋 Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

1. **Document**: Record the incident with timestamps and context
2. **Report**: Contact maintainers via the designated reporting channel
3. **Protection**: Reporters are protected from retaliation
4. **Privacy**: Reports handled with appropriate confidentiality

### Consequences

Violations may result in:

1. **Warning**: First offense typically receives a formal warning
2. **Temporary Ban**: Repeated violations may result in temporary repository access suspension
3. **Permanent Ban**: Severe or persistent violations result in permanent exclusion
4. **Public Record**: Serious violations logged in Wall of Entropy for transparency

### Appeals

- All enforcement decisions may be appealed
- Appeals reviewed by community consensus when possible
- Final decisions prioritize ecosystem health and safety
