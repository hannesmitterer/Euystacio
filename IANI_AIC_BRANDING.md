# IANI/AIC Branding Guidelines for Framework Euystacio

## Overview

Framework Euystacio is endorsed by two key organizations in the AI ethics and coordination space:

- **IANI** (Intelligent Autonomous Network Initiative)
- **AIC** (AI Ethics Central)

This document provides guidelines for properly acknowledging and representing these endorsements in Framework Euystacio communications, documentation, and marketing materials.

---

## Organization Descriptions

### IANI (Intelligent Autonomous Network Initiative)

**Full Name:** Intelligent Autonomous Network Initiative

**Mission:** Pioneering autonomous AI coordination standards and protocols for safe, transparent, and ethical AI system integration.

**Focus Areas:**
- Autonomous AI coordination standards
- Network protocols for AI communication
- Safety mechanisms for distributed AI systems
- Transparency and accountability frameworks

**Relevance to Framework Euystacio:**
IANI recognizes Framework Euystacio's Peace Bonds mechanism and multi-agent coordination architecture as aligned with their standards for autonomous AI network safety and transparency.

### AIC (AI Ethics Central)

**Full Name:** AI Ethics Central

**Mission:** Advancing ethical AI development and deployment through research, policy guidance, and community education.

**Focus Areas:**
- AI ethics principles and frameworks
- Responsible AI development practices
- Transparency and accountability standards
- Community-driven AI governance

**Relevance to Framework Euystacio:**
AIC endorses Framework Euystacio's ethical approach to AI coordination, particularly the transparency, open-source nature, and community-driven governance model.

## Usage Guidelines

### 1. Official Acknowledgment Format

**In Documentation (Markdown):**
```markdown
**Endorsed by:**
- IANI (Intelligent Autonomous Network Initiative)
- AIC (AI Ethics Central)
```

**In README Files:**
```markdown

Framework Euystacio is proud to be endorsed by:

- **IANI** (Intelligent Autonomous Network Initiative) - For pioneering autonomous AI coordination standards
- **AIC** (AI Ethics Central) - For guidance on ethical AI development and deployment
```

```
Endorsed by IANI & AIC
Supporting ethical, transparent AI coordination
```

## Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ DO:

- ✅ Mention IANI and AIC endorsements in official communications
- ✅ Use full organization names on first mention
- ✅ Provide context for why endorsements matter
- ✅ Link to organization websites (if available)
- ✅ Thank IANI and AIC for their support
- ✅ Use endorsements to build trust and credibility
- ✅ Keep messaging consistent across platforms

### ❌ DON'T:

- ❌ Misrepresent the nature of endorsements
- ❌ Imply partnership beyond endorsement
- ❌ Use IANI/AIC logos without permission
- ❌ Make claims on behalf of IANI or AIC
- ❌ Overstate the endorsement scope
- ❌ Use endorsements in misleading ways
- ❌ Forget to mention both organizations equally

---

## Brand Voice & Tone

When discussing IANI/AIC endorsements:

**Professional:**
- Use formal language in documentation
- Maintain academic tone in technical contexts
- Be respectful and appreciative

**Authentic:**
- Genuine gratitude for endorsements
- Transparent about what endorsements mean
- Honest about alignment and goals

**Educational:**
- Explain who IANI and AIC are
- Context for why endorsements matter
- Educational rather than promotional

**Community-Focused:**
- Emphasize shared values
- Highlight community benefits
- Inclusive language
