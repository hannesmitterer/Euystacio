## 🔑 Key Concepts

### Decentralization
No single point of control or failure. Power distributed across many participants.

### IPFS (InterPlanetary File System)
Content-addressed storage where files are referenced by their cryptographic hash, not location.

### Blockchain Anchoring
Creating immutable, timestamped proof that data existed at a specific time.

### Open Source
Software and knowledge whose "recipe" is publicly available for anyone to use, modify, and share.

### Permanence
Building systems that outlast any individual organization or institution.

## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions

### Is this really free?
Yes, forever. Open-source licenses ensure it stays free.

### Will this work offline?
Yes! Most Seedbringer tools are designed to work offline and sync when connected.

### Do I need to be a programmer?
No. Many roles don't require coding: documentation, translation, community organizing, testing, teaching.

### How is this different from corporate platforms?
Corporate platforms can change terms, raise prices, or shut down. Seedbringer is decentralized—no one can control or destroy it.

### Is my data private?
Public by default (for transparency), but encryption is available for sensitive data.

### How much does it cost?
Mostly free. Some blockchain operations cost pennies. IPFS storage is free or very cheap (~$5/month).
