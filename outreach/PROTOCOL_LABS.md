## Libp2p Community Outreach

**Subject:** Framework Euystacio: P2P AI Agent Coordination with libp2p

---

Dear Libp2p Community,

I'm building Framework Euystacio, an open-source AI coordination platform, and planning to integrate libp2p for peer-to-peer agent communication.

**Why Libp2p for AI?**

AI agents need to:
- Communicate across NATs
- Discover peers dynamically
- Subscribe to event streams
- Maintain secure connections

Libp2p solves all of these!

**Planned Integration:**

- Transport: TCP + WebSockets
- NAT traversal: Hole punching
- Discovery: mDNS + DHT
- Pubsub: GossipSub
- Security: Noise protocol

**Questions for the Community:**

1. Best practices for long-lived connections?
