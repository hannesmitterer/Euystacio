## Collapse Risk Reduction

### Methodology

**Collapse Risk Factors:**
1. **Single Point of Failure** (30% of risk)
2. **Financial Sustainability** (25% of risk)
3. **Security Vulnerabilities** (20% of risk)
4. **Knowledge Loss** (15% of risk)
5. **Community Fragmentation** (10% of risk)

**Funding Impact on Risk:**

| Monthly Funding | Collapse Risk | Risk Level | Sustainability |
|----------------|---------------|------------|----------------|
| $0 | 80% | Critical | Weeks |
| $400 | 45% | High | 3-6 months |
| $800 | 20% | Medium | 12 months |
| $1,600 | 10% | Low | 24+ months |
| $3,200+ | <5% | Minimal | Multi-year |

**Formula:**
```
Collapse Risk = 
  (1 - Funding_Factor) * 0.25 +
  (1 - Redundancy_Factor) * 0.30 +
  (1 - Security_Factor) * 0.20 +
  (1 - Documentation_Factor) * 0.15 +
  (1 - Community_Factor) * 0.10

Where each factor ranges from 0 (worst) to 1 (best)
```
