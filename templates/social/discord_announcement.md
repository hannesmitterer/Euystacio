## Channel-Specific Guidelines

- Use Template 1 (Formal)

- Update monthly with Template 5

### #general
- Use Template 2 (Casual)
- More conversational
- Engage with replies

- Link to issues and PRs

### #support
- Provide detailed links
- Answer questions thoroughly
- Use bot commands for quick info

## Engagement Best Practices

**DO:**
- Respond to all questions within 24 hours
- Thank contributors publicly (with permission)
- Share updates regularly
- Be transparent about challenges
- Engage authentically

**DON'T:**
- Spam announcements
- Pressure for donations
- Ignore concerns
- Over-promote
- Use excessive @everyone/@here

---

## Timing Strategy

**Best times to post:**
- Tuesday-Thursday
- 10 AM - 2 PM EST
- Avoid weekends for major announcements

**Update frequency:**
- Major announcements: As needed
- Progress updates: Monthly
- Quick wins: Weekly
- Respond to questions: Within 24h
